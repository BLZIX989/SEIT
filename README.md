# Spectral Emergence Information Theory (SEIT)

## A Unified Framework for the Emergence of Structure, Physics, Life, and Intelligence

### Overview

Spectral Emergence Information Theory (SEIT) proposes that reality is not fundamentally composed of particles, fields, spacetime, or matter. Instead, reality emerges from the organization, transformation, and persistence of distinctions within an underlying spectral structure.

SEIT begins with a simple question:

> What persists?

From this perspective, atoms, stars, organisms, ecosystems, minds, civilizations, and physical laws are manifestations of the same underlying process: the persistence of distinguishable structures through transformation.

$$
