# Master TOE Closure Matrix

Narrative companion to `MASTER_TOE_CLOSURE_MATRIX.csv`. Re-assesses every branch from
`MASTER_PHYSICS_CLOSURE_MATRIX.csv` in light of this campaign's corpus-mining pass.

## Summary: what changed

Of 17 branches previously assessed, **this campaign changes the closure verdict for none of
them** — no branch moved from OPEN to CLOSED, and no branch moved from a closed-like status to
OPEN. What changed is the *evidence base* for several branches:

- **Symmetry**: gained one genuine external-mathematics reference (the Constraint Necessity
  Theorem, `DTC-FS-1`) but remains OPEN — the theorem does not itself supply a UOC Lagrangian.
- **Conservation**: gained the explicit, honest confirmation from the corpus's own author that the
  Generalized Noether Conjecture is unproved and why — remains OPEN, now with the exact
  obstruction on record rather than merely absent.
- **Quantum**: gained a directly relevant *contradiction* — Master Equation Codex's claim that the
  Schrödinger equation is the continuum limit of the graph spectral problem is inconsistent with
  this project's own FC-005 finding. Status unchanged (VERIFIED for the 1 existing guardrail step
  only) but the corpus's own broader claim for this branch is now on record as rejected.
- **Constants**: two specific claimed closures (fine-structure constant, electron mass) were
  actively tested and **falsified** this campaign — a genuine negative result, not merely an
  absence of evidence.
- **Gauge/Standard Model**: two corpus documents claiming to derive SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) were
  assessed; neither survives as a derivation from this project's own primitives. The real,
  established gauge group itself remains preserved as external physics per the governing
  instruction.
- **Early-universe/Cosmology**: gained one flagged, unresolved, structurally legitimate prediction
  candidate (SEIT v2's axion/GW/soliton triplet) — status unchanged (OPEN) pending verification.

## Why no branch closed

Every closure attempt this campaign traced back to the same root obstruction documented in
`MASTER_TOE_THEOREM.md`: a derivation from a constraint/selection structure C to a physical result
R cannot be carried out if C has not been independently pinned down first. Every corpus document
that appears, on its surface, to derive geometry, gauge structure, or physical constants does so
by presupposing a specific starting structure (a distinction graph, a hypergraph, a topos) that
is never itself independently justified — the same `SELECTION-SIGMA` obstruction this project's
own canonical code has carried, honestly, since before this campaign began.
