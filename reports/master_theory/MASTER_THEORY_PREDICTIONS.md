# MASTER THEORY PREDICTIONS

Per the governing anti-fiction rule, this file lists only predictions that follow from actually
verified or calculated content in this repository. It does not manufacture predictions to give
H1-H4 the appearance of a productive outcome.

## 1. What H1-H4 do NOT license
None of H1 (selection undefined), H2 (D+ non-local), H3 (higher-mode non-convergence persists),
or H4 (specific gauge-intersection claim falsified) produces a new physical prediction. A FAIL,
an OPEN, or a FALSIFIED result on a closure hypothesis is not itself a testable prediction about
nature — it is a statement about what this repository's own construction can and cannot
currently support. No numbers (masses, couplings, cosmological parameters) are generated by any
of H1-H4, and none should be reported as if they were.

## 2. The one branch that does license a prediction: the graph-Laplacian spectral cascade
The already-VERIFIED chain `G → L=D-W → Spec(L) → R(t)=e^{-tL} → P_ker(L)` (Test 1, unchanged by
this campaign) is a mathematical fact about weighted graphs, not a physical theory, so it
licenses no independent physical prediction beyond its own domain (spectral graph theory is
correctly reproduced, per `proof_registry.json`).

## 3. Candidate prediction noted, not endorsed, in the historical corpus
Phase B's audit of the pre-compiler document corpus found one candidate prediction set — SEIT
v2 section VI's axion-mass / gravitational-wave-frequency / soliton-core-radius triplet — judged
"structurally the most legitimate falsifiable claim found in the corpus" because it uses a real
formula form with internally consistent arithmetic rather than a reverse fit to an already-known
answer. It was **not** independently re-derived, computed, or checked against observational
bounds by this campaign (H1-H4 did not touch it) and remains status OPEN /
untested-this-campaign. It is listed here for completeness, not as a result of this campaign's
own work.

## 4. Falsification conditions already on record (not new)
`MASTER_THEORY_FALSIFICATION_REGISTRY.json` carries the falsification protocols already
executed prior to and during this campaign (representation-invariance test on Spec(L), the
Fisher-Rao/Lorentzian-signature obstruction, the eigenvalue-uniqueness counterexample, and this
campaign's own `FALS-H4-G2-TRIALITY-RANK-OBSTRUCTION`). These are tests this project's own
candidate structures were subjected to and, in the H4 case and the Fisher-Rao case, failed —
they are not predictions about observable phenomena outside this repository's own constructions.

## 5. Conclusion
This campaign generates **zero new falsifiable physical predictions**. This is stated plainly
rather than papered over: producing a prediction from H1-H4's negative results would require
inventing content none of the four tests actually supplies, which the governing anti-fiction
rule explicitly forbids.
